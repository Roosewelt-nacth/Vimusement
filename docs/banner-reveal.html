The community banner

The #reveal section on the home page. It has two stages, and it moves between them on its own.

1 · Dormant

Until the date in the config, the whole section is hidden. Nothing renders, nothing animates. Visitors don't see it at all.

2 · Live

On reveal.opensOn the section appears by itself, no deploy needed. Then:

  • as the section scrolls up the screen, a 14×9 grid assembles into the Victorians “V” (ambient, driven by scroll position)
  • visitors tap tiles to place their own, and the grid fills toward reveal.goalTaps
  • each visitor's contribution is remembered in their browser until the shared backend counter is added
  • when the goal is reached the grid locks into a full glowing “V” and the caption changes

The config

All of it lives in years/2026.config.js:

/* Banner reveal. Hidden until opensOn, then it wakes up on its own. */
reveal: {
  live: false,                        // force on now, for a look before the date
  opensOn: "2026-12-19T00:00:00+05:30", // when it turns itself on (IST)
  goalTaps: 500                        // tiles placed together
}

To see it before the date without touching the config, add ?reveal=preview to the URL. Logic and rendering are in assets/js/modules/mosaic.js; styling is under /* BANNER REVEAL */ in assets/css/pages/home.css.
