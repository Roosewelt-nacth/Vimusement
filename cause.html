How support reaches someone

If a family is going through a hard stretch

This support is for families within the parish — people known here, often through years of shared Sundays and everyday kindnesses. If things are difficult, or you know someone who could use help, you're welcome to speak with a Victorians Youth team member or the parish priest directly. Every request is followed up with a quiet, respectful background check before anything is decided — enough to be fair to everyone the fund supports, and no more.
