# Website launch, after mass

## Setup (10 minutes before the end of mass)

1. Connect the laptop to the projector and the internet (church Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot).
2. Open **https://victoriansyouth.github.io/Vimusement2k26/launch.html** in Chrome. Press **F** (or F11) for full screen.
3. Rehearse once: press **Space** to start, then **R** to reset to the start.
4. If the hall has speakers, turn the laptop volume up for the chime. **M** switches the sound on or off.
5. Leave the start screen up: the brass lamp, unlit, with the gold **LIGHT THE LAMP** button.

What happens: a big **3 · 2 · 1** (the hall counts along), the lamp lights wick by wick, and the carnival behind it warms up in gold: the string of lights, the Ferris wheel, the carousel, the stalls and the church windows. Then "Vimusement 2026 is live.". After about 11 seconds it settles on the QR code, the pledge and the live supporters count. **Explore the website** (already selected, so just press Enter) opens the homepage on the projector.

## MC script

**Before the press (about 45 seconds)**

> Before you leave, one more thing. This year, Vimusement has a new home online.
> On it you'll find everything about the fair on the 25th of October: the programme and a map of the grounds, the movie line-up, the Lucky Draw prizes, and ways to volunteer or run a stall.
> And most importantly, it shows where every rupee goes. This year we are announcing it up front: forty percent for the education of the poor, thirty percent for medical and other needs, and thirty percent for the Emergency Fund. Every rupee in and out will be shown on a public ledger.
> To launch it, may I request Father to come forward and light the lamp. When the numbers appear, let's all count down together!

**Father presses LIGHT THE LAMP; everyone counts 3, 2, 1**

**After the reveal (about 30 seconds)**

> Vimusement 2026 is live! Take out your phones and scan the code on the screen, or look up Victorians Youth on Instagram, where the link is on our story.
> If you'd like to give, you can do it right there on the site, and you'll see the number of supporters on the screen go up.
> Thank you, Father, and thank you all. See you on the 25th of October.
