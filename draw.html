    How it works

    1. Choose how many tickets and pay by UPI, or buy cash at any counter on the grounds.
    2. Once we confirm the payment, your designed tickets (your name and a unique number) are emailed to you as a PDF to keep or print.
    3. On the night, numbers are drawn live on stage. Winners are also contacted directly.
    4. Win or not, your ₹50 already did its job. It’s a gift to the cause.
