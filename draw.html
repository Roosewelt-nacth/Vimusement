The prizes

    Prizes are announced closer to the date. The draw is run live on stage, and every sold, confirmed ticket is in the hat.

    How it works

    1. Choose how many tickets and pay by UPI, or buy cash at any counter on the grounds.
    2. Once we confirm the payment, your designed tickets (your name and a unique number) are emailed to you as a PDF to keep or print.
    3. On the night, numbers are drawn live on stage. Winners are also contacted directly.
    4. Win or not, your ₹50 already did its job. It’s a gift to the cause.

    Questions people ask

    What if I don't win?

    Every ticket, sold and confirmed, already went to the cause — the draw is just the fun part on top. There's no losing, only maybe-winning.

    Can I buy more than one ticket?

    Yes — as many as you like, up to the online limit. Each one is a separate number in the hat, so more tickets means better odds.

    I don't want to pay online — can I still enter?

    Yes — buy cash at any counter on the grounds on the day, and your ticket is issued to you on the spot.

    How will I know if I've won?

    Winning numbers are called live on stage on the night, and winners are also contacted directly using the details on their ticket.
