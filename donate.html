Before you give

Is this actually safe?

Yes — you pay the parish's own UPI ID directly, in your own banking app. There's no third-party gateway, no card details anywhere, and no middleman holding your money at any point.

Where exactly does it go?

Straight to the parish's fund for scholarships, the medical-emergency fund, and hardship support — decided by the committee, published in full after the event. See The Cause for last year's numbers.

Do I get a receipt or confirmation?

Yes — once your payment is confirmed, you'll get an email and, if you added a number, a text too. Usually within a day.

Does everyone see how much I gave?

No — amounts are never shown publicly, only names, and only if you tick the box. You can also give without your name appearing at all.
