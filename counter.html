Vimusement counter

Issue lucky-draw tickets & record cash donations

How to use this

  1. Log in with your username and today’s desk key — the committee gives these out at the start of your shift.
  2. Pick a tab: Lucky Draw for a ticket sale, Cash Donation for a cash gift, or Confirm UPI once you see someone’s online payment land in the bank app.
  3. Fill in the buyer or donor’s details and submit. The ticket numbers or reference appear on screen — read them out or write them on the ticket before the next sale.
  4. The bar at the top keeps a running total to hand over — that’s what you should have in the cash box when your shift ends.
  5. Tap End shift when you’re done. It resets the tally and logs you out for the next volunteer.
